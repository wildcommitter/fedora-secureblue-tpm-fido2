Qubes OS alternative · comparison

Qubes OS is the gold standard. Here's what we took from it.

Qubes OS isolates everything in Xen domains. That's the strongest desktop security model in production. scroll-secure isn't that, and won't pretend to be. It takes the Qubes ideas that work on a normal Linux desktop and makes them the default on hardened Fedora Atomic.

Who should pick which

Pick Qubes OS if a compromised browser must not be able to reach your password manager, or if you face targeted attackers who will chain exploits. Compartmentalization at the hypervisor level is the only real answer to that, and Qubes does it best.

Pick scroll-secure if you want a fast daily driver with a real GPU, and the threat you care most about is physical: a stolen or seized machine, or someone with ten minutes alone with it. Your boot chain, your keys and your disk unlock stay under your control.

And if you run Qubes OS today: the two-token unlock design may still be worth a read.
